How to Avoid Buffering

Internet service providers sometimes slow down streaming traffic. To prevent this, use Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1). Also set your player’s buffer or cache to 5,000 milliseconds. These steps help keep the stream steady.

For best results, check your internet speed. A stable connection of at least 25 Mbps is recommended for 4K streams. Research from Carnegie Mellon shows that proper DNS settings can reduce latency, which is useful for live streams.
